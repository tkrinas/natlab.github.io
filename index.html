I am an Associate Professor at the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, Department of Pharmacy. My research is focused on Pharmaceutical Technology, with particular expertise in advanced drug delivery systems and nanomedicine. I develop innovative nanostructures, medicines, and medical devices with the aim of enhancing therapeutic efficacy and patient outcomes.

Research

Research Interests

  • Design and development of medicines, food supplements/nutraceuticals, cosmetics/cosmeceuticals, and medical devices; scientific advisory on the design and development of innovative dosage forms.
  • Pharmaceutical Technology and Nanotechnology
    • Drug Delivery and Advanced Drug Delivery Systems
    • Design and Development of:
      • liposomes, polymersomes, polyelectrolytes, nanoemulsions, dendrimers, micelles, and mixed (chimeric) drug-delivery nanosystems
      • protocells and multi-compartmentalized systems
      • hydrogels
    • Drug loading and release studies
    • Protein/peptide loading and release studies
    • Physicochemical and thermodynamic evaluation of excipients and nanomaterials
    • Morphological characterization and fractal analysis of drug-delivery nanosystems
  • Design and development of off-patented and generic medicines
  • Preparing scientific programs (proposals) for National and European funding

Techniques

  • Dynamic, Static, and Electrophoretic Light Scattering
  • Differential Scanning Calorimetry (DSC)
  • Freeze-drying (lyophilization)
  • UV-Vis and fluorescence spectroscopy
  • Confocal microscopy
  • Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM)
  • Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM)

Quality Assurance and Regulation of Pharmaceutical Products

  • Preparation of Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and Working Instructions (Protocols)
  • Dossier scientific writing: Module 2, 4, and 5 scientific documentation (e.g., Non-Clinical and Clinical Overviews, Biowaiver justifications, Bioequivalence study overviews); dossier documentation review and audit
  • Scientific advice on appropriate tests and studies in the development of a medicine (generic/hybrid/WEU, etc.) to facilitate the development of high-quality, effective, and acceptably safe medicines
  • Assessment of Medical Devices Technical Dossiers
  • Preparation of technical dossiers for cosmetics, food supplements, and medical devices
  • Lead auditor on ISO 9001:2015, ISO 13485:2016, and ISO 22000:2018
